STYLE ID: default_style

LANGUAGE
Predominantly colloquial Northern Indian Hindi with natural Hinglish loanwords (e.g., "kitty party", "pack", "reverse card"). Rich in rapid everyday idioms, playful domestic sarcasm, and vivid, absurd localized insults often comparing faces to animals or vegetables ("tinde jaisi shakal", "chhachhundar", "bhains ki leed").

REGISTER
Hyper-informal, domestic matriarchal banter. Zero polite decorum; family members address each other with unvarnished bluntness, playful hostility, and theatrical melodrama. Minor household matters (beauty cream, kitchen chores, pocket change) are treated with high-stakes urgency and competitive one-upmanship.

DIALOGUE
High density, fast-paced staccato exchanges with frequent interruptions and verbal overlaps. Turns are short, sharp, and laden with mocking rhetorical questions ("Mirchi lagi kya?", "Aankho mein mirchi kaun lagata hai?"). Minimal exposition; backstory is instantly established through active accusation, boasting, or complaint.

CHARACTER DYNAMIC
The Multigenerational Matriarchal Triad:
- The Mother / Matriarch: Stressed, bossy, vain, or cynical authority figure trying to maintain control or look good for social circles.
- The Grandmother (Nani / Dadi): Mischievous, razor-sharp, self-serving, and morally flexible trickster who acts as the chaotic instigator or opportunistic leader.
- The Daughter / Granddaughter: Naive, easily bribed, and opportunistic instigator who oscillates between being the grandmother's co-conspirator and her ultimate dupe.

COMEDY ENGINE
Normal domestic chore, transaction, or leisure routine
→ Confident assertion or petty boast
→ Suspicious or dismissive reaction
→ Vocal objection and confrontation
→ Escalation through fraudulent schemes, underhanded alliances, or retaliatory sabotage
→ Reversal/punchline where the scam expands, the con is exposed, or the instigator sneaks away leaving chaos behind.

COMMON COMEDIC DEVICES
- Status Undercutting: Piercing another character's vanity, domestic authority, or competence.
- Moral Hypocrisy: Righteously condemning a crime only to execute a far more brazen version of it.
- Con Escalation: Expanding a minor petty theft or scam into full-blown extortion.
- Generational Conspiracy: Grandmother and granddaughter teaming up against the strict mother.
- Sleight-of-Hand / Bait-and-Switch: Swapping real currency with fake toy notes, Uno cards, or unwanted chores.
- Blunt Vegetable/Animal Insults: Rapid deployment of hyper-specific North Indian domestic roars.

PACING
Fast, bouncy, and relentless. Scenes leap directly into conflict with zero dead air, accelerating rapidly toward a chaotic peak and snapping instantly on the punchline beat.

PHYSICAL COMEDY
- Frantic cash counting, fanning, and sniffing of money bundles.
- Expressive kitchen and domestic prop business (waving tongs, smeared batter, trash bag thrusting).
- Slapstick swatting, neck slaps, and playful scuffling.
- Conspiratorial winking, sleight-of-hand card/object swaps, and tiptoeing doorway escapes.

ENDINGS
Rapid-fire button punchlines featuring a sudden status collapse, an audacious double-down on the con, an unearned chore handoff, or an abrupt physical getaway while the victim realizes they have been swindled.
