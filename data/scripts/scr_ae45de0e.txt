**INT. LIVING ROOM - EVENING**

MUMMY enters, slumping her purse on the sofa, exhausted. 

On the center table, DADI and PIHU are furiously wiping their fingers on tissues around three empty, foil takeout boxes.

MUMMY
Bhookh ke maare meri aantein sookh gayi hain! Kuch bana hai ghar mein ya zinda chaba jaaun kisi ko?

DADI
(Sweetly, gesturing to the table)
Dekh, aate hi chillana shuru! Tere liye special royal Shahi Paneer mangwaya hai Swiggy se!

PIHU
Haan Mummy, humne socha aap thak ke aaogi toh warm welcome hona chahiye!

Mummy smiles, touched. She sits down, grabs a spoon, and opens the plastic container. Her smile vanishes.

MUMMY
Yeh... isme paneer kahan hai?

DADI
Andhi ho gayi hai kya? Saamne toh tair raha hai!

Mummy violently stirs the container. The spoon scrapes empty plastic bottom.

MUMMY
Tair raha hai?! Yeh toh peeli nadi beh rahi hai bas! Ek tukda nahi hai paneer ka! Ek single cube nahi!

PIHU
(Defensive)
Mummy, science ke hisaab se paneer mein toxic fat hota hai. Aapki Umar mein seedha heart attack!

DADI
Humne apni jaan jokhim mein daal ke saare cubes chaba liye! Teri sehat ke liye kurbani di hai humne!

MUMMY
Kurbani di hai ya loot machayi hai, tote jaisi shakal waliyon?!

PIHU
(Flashing Swiggy app on phone)
Woh sab chhodo, total bill aaya hai baarah sau rupaye. Aapka hissa banta hai aath sau!

MUMMY
(Voice cracking in disbelief)
Aath sau rupaye?! Sirf paani jaisi gravy peene ke main aath sau doon?!

DADI
Kyun na de? Asli mehenge masale toh gravy mein hi ghule hain na! Paneer toh bas maida aur doodh ka thokka hota hai!

MUMMY
Khaoge tum dono aur bill bharega mera baap?! 

PIHU
Nana ji ko beech mein mat laao, unka Google Pay waise bhi block rehta hai! 

DADI
Aur sun, teen sau rupaye delivery boy ki tip ke alag se jod lena. Us bechare ne chauthi manzil tak tere masale chadhaaye hain!

MUMMY
(Lunging forward, grabbing the spoon like a weapon)
Abhi batati hoon delivery boy ki tip! Ek phooti kaudi nahi milegi!

Dadi instantly jumps up from the sofa with Olympic agility and shoves the stack of greasy, empty plastic boxes right into Mummy’s chest.

DADI
(Heading swiftly towards the bedroom)
Theek hai, bill mat de! Par dhabba achhe se dho ke rakhna, kal isme dahi jamani hai!

Dadi and Pihu bolt into the bedroom and SLAM the door shut.

MUMMY
(Screaming at the door)
Kamineeeyooo!

**BLACKOUT.**
