**INT. LIVING ROOM - DAY**

MUMMY sits in front of a blinding ring light wearing heavy jewelry and oversized sunglasses, staring frantically at her frozen laptop. 

DAUGHTER kicks the blinking Wi-Fi router under the TV.

MUMMY
Arre yeh Wi-Fi ka dabba laal batti kyu jala raha hai?! Meri ‘South Delhi Royal Queens’ wali kitty party shuru hone wali hai!

DAUGHTER
Router ka antim sanskar ho gaya, mummy. Padosi ki billi ne taar chaba li.

DADI reclines on the sofa, scrolling YouTube at lightning speed.

DADI
Aur bana billi jaisi shakal! Yahan hamara 5G rocket ki tarah udd raha hai.

MUMMY
(Pleading, hands folded)
Mummy, apna hotspot on karo na jaldi! Pammi aur Sunita mera naya choker dekhne ka wait kar rahi hain!

DADI
(Smirks, holding up phone)
Sarkari daftaar nahi hai yeh! Sau rupaye per minute. Cash down, advance payment!

DAUGHTER
Waah Dadi! Ambani bhi aapse tution lene aayega!

MUMMY
(Thrusts a 100-rupee note at Dadi)
Yeh lo sau rupaye! Jaldi password batao, Zoom connect ho raha hai!

DADI snatches the note, sniffs it loudly, and tucks it into her blouse.

DADI
Password likh—‘MummyChhachhundarHai’.

MUMMY
Aapka dimaag kharab ho gaya hai kya?!

DADI
Small letters mein likh, varna agla minute shuru ho jayega! Tik-tik ek... tik-tik do...

Mummy furiously punches keys. The Zoom chime rings out. 

MUMMY
(Sweet, fake British-accented voice to laptop)
Hii girls! So sorry late ho gayi, mera private jet traffic mein phas gaya tha—

*BEEP!* The video call freezes instantly. Mummy is stuck on screen with her mouth wide open.

MUMMY
(Dropping accent, screaming)
Arre! Freeze kyu ho gayi main?! Mummy, hotspot band kyu kiya?!

DADI
(Calmly checking wristwatch)
Sau rupaye ka saatth second khatam! Agla minute do sau ka hai kyunki tu international jhooth bol rahi hai.

DAUGHTER
(Flashing a 500-rupee note)
Dadi! Mujhse paanch sau le lo, mujhe reels dekhni hain!

DADI
(Snatches the 500 note, kicks Mummy’s chair away)
Deal done! Aur sun tinde jaisi shakal wali, laptop band kar aur kitchen se chai bana ke la!

MUMMY
Mummy?! Meri kitty party chal rahi hai!

DADI
(Grinning to Daughter)
Chai ke saath do mirchi pakode laayegi, toh das second ka bonus hotspot free!
