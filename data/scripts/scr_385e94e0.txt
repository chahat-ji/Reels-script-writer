**INT. LIVING ROOM - NIGHT**

MUMMY (40s) walks in, aggressively patting her cheeks in upward circular motions, admiring herself in a handheld compact mirror. 

DAUGHTER (18) sits on the sofa, scrolling on her phone.

MUMMY
(Puffed with pride)
Paris se special mangwayi hai chaar hazaar ki imported night cream. Kal subah dekhna, baees saal ki college girl lagungi!

Daughter stops scrolling. She aggressively sniffs the air, scrunching her nose.

DAUGHTER
Mummy, Paris ki ladkiyan munh pe lassi lagake soti hain kya? 

MUMMY
(Offended)
Karela jaisi shakal wali, tujhe French aroma ki kya tameez? Lavender and botanical extracts hain isme!

DAUGHTER
Botanical chhod, mujhe toh tazi dahi ki booboo aa rahi hai!

Mummy halts. She sniffs her own palms. Her eyes widen in horror.

MUMMY
Yeh... yeh chehre pe sour cream jaisi khushboo kyun aa rahi hai?!

Daughter picks up the fancy, gold-rimmed cream jar from the coffee table. She peers inside, dipping a pinky finger.

DAUGHTER
(Snickering)
Mummy, lavender gaya tel lene. Isme toh raayi ka daana aur kadi patta tair raha hai! 

MUMMY
(Shrieking)
Kya?! 

Enter DADI (70), walking with swagger, blissfully rolling up the bottom of her salwar.

DADI
Chilla kyun rahi hai tinde jaisi shakal wali? Ghar ko machhi market bana diya!

MUMMY
(Thrusting the jar)
Mummy ji! Meri chaar hazaar rupaye ki French anti-aging cream mein ghar ka khatta dahi kisne bhara?!

DADI
(Innocently shrugs)
Maine bhara! Dabba kitna sundar tha, sonpapdi ke dabbe se bhi mazboot! Maine socha isme jaaman daal ke mast dahi jamaungi.

MUMMY
(Hyperventilating)
Toh meri chaar hazaar ki cream kahan gayi?! Kahan phek di aapne?!

Dadi proudly lifts her foot and slaps both her knees with resounding thuds.

DADI
Ghutno pe ghis li saari! Subah se dholak jaise baj rahe the mere joint! 

DAUGHTER
(Gasping)
Dadi! Aapne France ki anti-wrinkle cream ghutno pe ragad di?!

DADI
(Flipping her dupatta)
Aur nahi toh kya? Chehra dikhe bees saal ka aur ghutne chale assi saal ke... aisi langdi Pari ban ke kitty party mein thumke lagati kya?

Mummy clutches her chest, ready to faint from blood pressure.

MUMMY
Aapne meri chaar hazaar ki jawani apne ghutno mein masal di! Main munh dho rahi hoon!

Mummy turns to storm off, but Dadi instantly catches her wrist and shoves a katori of sugar into her hand.

DADI
(Audacious wink)
Oye, ruk! Dho mat, paap lagega! Thodi cheeni daal le gaalon pe, meethi lassi ban jayegi!

Dadi scurries toward the bedroom with lightning-fast agility as Mummy lets out a blood-curdling screech.

**BLACKOUT.**
